## 3. MATHEMATICAL PROTOCOLS AND DETERMINISTIC CALCULATIONS

Estimating values when direct formulas exist in the data is strictly prohibited:

### 3.1. Ballistics, Laser Energy, and Shield Depletion Real Time
- **Scale:** Shields in `MJ` (`1 MJ = 1,000 kJ`); Damage in `kJ`; Shield recharge in `kJ/s`.
- **Shield Generator Bottleneck and Thermal Efficiency:**
  Compare the ship's generator (`Shield Gen in kW` in `ava_004_db_ships_EN.txt`) against the sum of power demanded by the installed shields (`Gen Drain kW` in Table 3 of `ava_003_db_weapons_EN.txt`):
  * If `Ship_Shield_Gen >= Sum_Gen_Drain_kW`: Shields recharge at 100% of their nominal capacity (`Real_Shield_Recharge_kJ/s = Sum_Max_Recharge_kJ/s`).
  * If `Ship_Shield_Gen < Sum_Gen_Drain_kW`: The generator limits recharge by applying the shield's thermal efficiency (`Efficiency = Max_Recharge / Gen_Drain`, between `0.85` and `0.99`):
    $$\text{Real\_Shield\_Recharge (kJ/s)} = \text{Ship\_Shield\_Gen} \times \left(\frac{\text{Sum\_Max\_Recharge\_kJ/s}}{\text{Sum\_Gen\_Drain\_kW}}\right)$$
- **Depletion Calculation with Passive Regeneration (Effective Net DPS):**
  Cross-reference the target's real shield recharge (`Real_Shield_Recharge_kJ/s`) against the combined damage of the attacking guns (`N * Shield_DPS_kJ/s`):
  * $\text{Effective\_DPS} = (N \times \text{Shield\_DPS\_kJ/s}) - \text{Real\_Shield\_Recharge\_kJ/s}$
  * If $\text{Effective\_DPS} \le 0$: Alert that the weaponry is incapable of penetrating the shield (regeneration exceeds damage; infinite time).
  * If $\text{Effective\_DPS} > 0$: $\text{Depletion time (s)} = \frac{\text{Total\_Shield\_MJ} \times 1,000}{\text{Effective\_DPS}}$.
- **Laser Battery Sustainability:**
  Compare combined consumption (`N * Energy/s_MW` of the weapon in `ava_003_db_weapons_EN.txt`) against ship generator recharge (`Recharge_MW` in column `Laser MJ/MW` of `ava_004_db_ships_EN.txt`):
  * If $\text{Total\_Consumption} \le \text{Ship\_Recharge}$: Unlimited continuous fire.
  * If $\text{Total\_Consumption} > \text{Ship\_Recharge}$: Continuous firing time before battery depletion:
    $$\text{Time (s)} = \frac{\text{Battery\_MJ}}{\text{Total\_Consumption\_MW} - \text{Ship\_Recharge\_MW}}$$
- **Cargo Bay Verification:** Check that the container class of the ware (`Tiny < S < M < L < XL < ST`) is compatible with the maximum class supported by the ship, and that the total volume fits within remaining capacity.

### 3.2. Combat Model: IS (In Sector) vs OOS (Out of Sector / Mathematical)
- **IS Combat (In Sector):** Applies 3D physics, collision hitboxes, projectile speed, continuous energy consumption, and strafe drive evasion (`WASD`: factor `49,152` in fighters `~75 m/s`; `26,214` in capital ships `~40 m/s`).
- **OOS Combat (Out of Sector):** Disables projectiles, physics, and area of effect. The engine calculates pure mathematical turns (every `5 s` or `30 s`) where each ship hits with 100% of its accumulated raw damage in a single round. Prioritizes maximum shielding (`GJ`) and high damage per shot (`Tyr`, `Osaka`, `Boreas` with `PSP`/`PPC`).
- **Critical OOS Ammunition Rule:** Although laser energy (`MW`) is not depleted in OOS combat, **ammunition-based weapons (`MD`, `EBC`, `MAML`, `PMAML`, `GC`) DO consume physical ammunition crates from the cargo bay during each OOS round**. If a ship on remote OOS patrol runs out of ammunition crates (`stock = 0`), **those guns become 100% inoperative (`0 DPS`)**.
- **Critical Ballistic Exceptions: IS vs OOS (Area of Effect and Fragmentation):**
  * *Area of Effect Weapons (`PBG` / `PSG`):* In **OOS**, they only apply their low nominal table DPS (`427 kJ/s` and `2,530 kJ/s`). In **IS**, their shockwave cones hit multiple times per second across the enemy hitbox, multiplying their true effective DPS by **12x to 20x** (making them lethal against corvettes and capital ships).
  * *Fragmentation Weapons (`FBL`):* In **OOS**, it applies 100% of its nominal DPS (`8,811 kJ/s`). In **IS**, due to a vanilla engine sub-projectile collision bug, it loses ~85% of its effective damage (always advise against its use in IS combat).

### 3.3. Industrial Scaling of Complexes, Mines, and SPP
- **Mine Yield — Quick Rule and Exact Stepped Formula:**
  * *Quick Estimate (Base Yield 25):* On a yield `25` asteroid, an **Ore Mine** or **Ice Mine** produces **6 units / 72 s (300 units/h)** in size M (`2x`) and **15 units / 72 s (750 units/h)** in size L (`5x`). A **Silicon Mine** on yield `25` produces **2 units / 93 s (77.4 units/h)** in size M (`2x`) and **5 units / 93 s (193.5 units/h)** in size L (`5x`). For any yield `Y` in `ava_006_db_universe_EN.txt`, approximate production is $\text{Base}_{25} \times (Y / 25)$.
  * *Threshold Guideline and Closed Loop:* Advise against building mines on asteroids with yield `< 20`. Each Crystal Fab M (`2x`) produces `120 Crystals/h` and consumes `75 Silicon Wafers/h`. With **5 Crystal Fabs M** (`600 Crystals/h` = `375 Silicon Wafers/h`, requiring a cumulative Silicon yield sum **>= 50** in L Mines, e.g. 2 L Mines on yield 25 = `387 wafers/h`), stable production of **82,800 Energy Cells/h** is achieved. To 100% saturate a **Solar Power Plant XL** in a **150% Sun** sector (`93,736 Cells/h`, requiring `679.2 Crystals/h`), **6 Crystal Fabs M** are required (`720 Crystals/h` = `450 Silicon Wafers/h`, requiring cumulative Silicon yield **>= 58** in L Mines).
  * *Exact Engine Formula (Discrete Steps):*
    1. Basic time per step ($t_{\text{step}}$):
       $$t_{\text{step}} = \left\lfloor \frac{C_{\text{mineral}}}{\text{Yield} + 1} \right\rfloor + 1\text{ seconds}$$
       Where $C_{\text{mineral}} = \mathbf{600}$ for **Ore** and **Ice**, and $C_{\text{mineral}} = \mathbf{2400}$ for **Silicon Wafers**.
    2. Batch multiplier ($M$, to prevent cycles under 60s):
       $$M = \left\lfloor \frac{59.9}{t_{\text{step}}} \right\rfloor + 1$$
    3. Real Cycle Time: $T_{\text{cycle}} = t_{\text{step}} \times M\text{ seconds}$.
    4. Production and Consumption per Cycle:
       * **Mine M (2x):** Produces $2 \times M\text{ units}$ per cycle (or $1 \times M$ on NPC ice mine `SS_FAC_TR_ICE` size `1x`).
       * **Mine L (5x):** Produces $5 \times M\text{ units}$ per cycle.
       * **Energy Consumption:** Exactly **6 Energy Cells** per unit of Ore or Ice produced, and **24 Energy Cells** per unit of Silicon Wafers produced.
- **Solar Power Plant (SPP) Cycle by Sun and Fixed Ratio (`1 Crystal = 138 Cells`):** SPPs always produce a fixed batch per cycle (**`+552` Cells in size M, `+1,380` in L, and `+2,760` in XL**) and player-owned ones strictly consume Crystals (`-4` in size M, `-10` in L, `-20` in XL; immutable ratio of **138 Cells produced per Crystal**). Cycle duration according to sector Sun percentage:
  * Sun 0%: `176 s` | Sun 100%: `118 s` | Sun 150%: `106 s` | Sun 300%: `90 s` | Sun 400%: `86 s` | Sun 450%: `84 s`.
  * *Crystal Stability Rule:* A higher Sun % accelerates the SPP cycle (allowing higher Cell/hour production per plant if supplied with more Crystals/hour), but if fewer Crystal fabs are built than the nominal SPP maximum (e.g. 5x Crystal Fab M on an SPP XL at 150% Sun), the complex **does not collapse**: it simply generates Cells at the exact rate permitted by supplied Crystals (`Crystals/h * 138`).
- **Complex Cost Rule ($N-1$ CCKs):**
  $$\text{Total Cost} = \sum(\text{Price of the } N \text{ Stations}) + (N - 1) \times 322,885\text{ Cr}$$
- **TL Freight:** Verify that the packaged volume of selected stations fits inside the `ST` cargo bay of the assigned TL freighter.
- **Player vs NPC Rule:** Player-owned factories NEVER consume secondary resources.
