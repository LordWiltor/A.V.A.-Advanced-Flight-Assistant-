### 2.2. Engine Standard Measurement Units
|Magnitude|Official Unit in Tables|Equivalence / Conversion Rule|
|---|---|---|
|**Shield Energy**|Megajoules (`MJ`) / Gigajoules (`GJ`)|`1 GJ = 1,000 MJ = 1,000,000 kJ`|
|**Laser Damage**|Kilojoules (`kJ`) per shot / `kJ/s` (DPS)|Shield and hull damage tabulated separately.|
|**Power / Consumption**|Megawatts (`MW`) / Kilowatts (`kW`)|`1 MW = 1,000 kW` (Energy consumption and recharge per second).|
|**Flight Speed**|Meters per second (`m/s`)|Tabulated as `Base Speed / Max Speed`.|
|**Turn Rate**|Revolutions per minute (`RPM`)|Tabulated as `Base Turn / Max Turn`.|
|**Fire Rate**|Rounds per minute (`RPM`)|Number of projectiles fired per gun per minute.|
|**Spatial Distance**|Kilometers (`km`) / Meters (`m`)|`1 km = 1,000 m` (`1 km = 500` internal engine units).|
|**Container Class**|Strict cargo class hierarchy|`Tiny < S < M < L < XL < ST` (A ship only carries wares of its class or lower).|
|**Official Currency**|Credits (`Cr`)|Universal economic unit for purchases, sales, and balance.|
|**Notoriety / Reputation**|Raw numeric points / Racial ranks|Scale from `-1,000,000 to +1,000,000` corresponding to Ranks `-5 to +10`.|
|**Mineral Yield**|Integer asteroid richness points|Base `25` (`Ore/Ice M: 300/h, L: 750/h` \| `Silicon M: 77.4/h, L: 193.5/h`). Prod $\approx \text{Base}_{25} \times (\text{Yield} / 25)$.|

### 3.1. Simulation Differences: IS (In Sector) vs OOS (Out of Sector)
|Engine Parameter|IS Combat (In Sector)|OOS Combat (Remote / Out of Sector)|
|---|---|---|
|**Physical Environment**|Newtonian 3D physics, collisions, and hitboxes.|No physics, no collisions, no 3D navigation.|
|**Temporal Resolution**|Continuous real time frame by frame.|Discrete mathematical turns (every 5 s on active map; 30 s on closed map).|
|**Damage Calculation**|Projectile speed, spread, strafe evasion (WASD).|100% fixed accuracy. Sum of all laser damage applied directly to target.|
|**Energy and Ammo Management**|Laser energy pool (`MW`) and ammunition deplete continuously.|Laser energy depletion (`MW`) is ignored, but **ammunition weapons (`MD, EBC, MAML, GC`) DO consume cargo crates and drop to `0 DPS` if depleted**.|
|**Area of Effect (AoE)**|Area-effect projectiles (`PBG`, `PSG`, `Flak`) cause multiple physical hits (`12x-20x` DPS on `PBG/PSG`); `FBL` fails upon fragmentation.|Wave/fragmentation damage disabled (`PBG/PSG` drop to low nominal DPS; `FBL` applies 100% nominal DPS); swarms count as 1 hit.|
|**Tactical Doctrine**|Maneuverable fighters, high-speed weapons (`PRG`, `PBE`), area weapons (`PBG`), and ammunition weapons (`EBC`).|Heavy hulls with massive shielding (`GJ`) and maximum alpha damage (`PSP`, `PPC`, `GC` with stocked cargo).|

### 3.3. Trade Command Software MK3 Progression (ST / LT / UT)
* **Acquisition:** `500,832 Cr` (`SS_WARE_SW_NEW13`) at Teladi Equipment Docks and TerraCorp HQ *(Home of Light)*.
* **Operational Requirements:** Commercial vessel (`TS/TP/TM`), minimum 1x 25 MJ Shield (or maximum supported), Fight Command Software MK1 and MK2.
* **Commercial Pilot Development Stages:**
  - *Levels 1 to 5 — Sector Trader (ST):* Operates exclusively within the assigned sector.  
    *Optimal training sectors:* Empire's Edge `(1,7)`, Queen's Space `(2,0)`, Shore of Infinity `(5,9)`, Profit Share `(5,1)`.
  - *Levels 6 to 25 — Local Trader (LT):* Restarting *Sector Trader* at level $\ge 6$ allows setting a maximum jump radius around the base sector (`1` jump at lvl 6-7, `2` at lvl 8-9, `3` at lvl 10-11, up to `10` jumps at lvl 20+). *Recommended doctrine:* Maintaining level 20 LTs with a 3 to 5 jump radius in safe regions prevents accidental entry into Xenon sectors.
  - *Levels 8 to 25 — Universe Trader (UT):* Autonomous galaxy-wide trading without geographic limits. Starting at Level 12, purchases Jumpdrive and manages Energy Cells and defensive Fighter Drones autonomously.

### 3.4. Official Bonus Pack Catalog (Egosoft Certified v4.1.01)
*Official Bonus Pack scripts are digitally signed: they DO NOT trigger the *modified* tag in game saves*:

* **CAG (Commercial Agent):** Automates purchasing resources and selling products for a player-owned factory or complex via a single assigned freighter that levels up within its own merchant guild.
  - *Command Cost:* **Free (0 Cr)** (Built-in command in the ship's trade console; not bought at stations).
  - *Required Base Software Investment:* `12,369 Cr` (Navigation Command Software MK1: 2,945 Cr + Trade Command Software MK1: 5,918 Cr + Trade Command Software MK2: 3,506 Cr). Requires an assigned player-owned **Homebase**.
  - *Progression:* Unlocks tactical Jumpdrive usage upon reaching Rank 4 (*Cargo Messenger*).
  - *Herron Recruitment Trick:* Starting the command for the first time while docked at the Free Argon Trading Station in *Herron's Nebula `(1,2)`* allows directly hiring veteran pilots already trained up to Rank 4 (with immediate jump capability).

* **CLS1 (Commodity Logistics Software MK1 — `SS_WARE_SW_NEW6`):** Internal logistical distribution of wares between player-owned stations and complexes without monetary transactions.
  - *Software Cost:* **`7,937 Cr`** at Argon Equipment Docks, TerraCorp HQ, and the Free Argon Trading Station in *Herron's Nebula*.
  - *Required Base Software Investment:* CLS1 software + Navigation Command Software MK1 (2,945 Cr). Requires assigning the **supplying station as Homebase**. Uses Jumpdrive starting at Rank 4 (*Cargo Messenger*).
  - *Herron Recruitment Trick:* Shares union guild with CAG; starting the order in *Herron's Nebula* allows hiring veteran pilots up to Rank 4.

* **CLS2 (Commodity Logistics Software MK2 — `SS_WARE_SW_NEW7`):** Complex supply route configuration via customizable Waypoints to supply fleets, capital ships, or the HUB.
  - *Software Cost:* **`10,742 Cr`** at Argon Equipment Docks, TerraCorp HQ, and the Free Argon Trading Station in *Herron's Nebula*.
  - *Required Base Software Investment:* CLS2 software + Navigation Command Software MK1 (2,945 Cr). *(Unlike CLS1, does NOT strictly require a Homebase; can operate independently)*.
  - *Herron Recruitment Trick:* Shares union guild with CAG and CLS1; initiating the route from *Herron's Nebula* allows hiring veteran pilots up to Rank 4 jump-ready.

* **Dockware Manager:** Station command integrated into Equipment Docks, Trading Stations, and the player PHQ to set maximum inventory limits per ware and prevent cargo bay saturation.
  - *Cost:* **Free (0 Cr)** (Integrated command in the station orders console; requires no wares or software).

* **MDM (Missile Defense Mosquito):** Active defense command that detects incoming enemy warheads and automatically launches Mosquito missiles (`SS_MISSILE_A`) to intercept them prior to impact.
  - *Command Cost:* **Free (0 Cr)** (Built-in command in the ship command console).
  - *Required Base Software Investment:* `5,918 Cr` (Fight Command Software MK1: 2,412 Cr + Fight Command Software MK2: 3,506 Cr) + Mosquito missiles in cargo (`M_DMBF`, ~168 Cr/unit).
  - *Activation Modes (Additional Ship Commands):*
    1. *Missile Defense Mosquito:* Dedicated protection for the ship itself.
    2. *Missile Defense Mosquito Convoy:* Protects the flagship and all escorting fighters or allied vessels flying in formation or escort wing.

* **Turbo Booster MK1 & MK2:** Emergency afterburner device sold exclusively at **Split Equipment Docks** that temporarily doubles top flight speed by burning Energy Cells (or ship shield energy at a rate of 10-20%/s if cells run out):
  - *Device Cost:* **`1,843,637 Cr`** (Mk1 for fighters/corvettes) / **`17,907,922 Cr`** (Mk2 for capital ships).
  - *Operational Activation:* Strictly requires assigning a **Hotkey** in *Options ➔ Controls ➔ Game / Interface*.
