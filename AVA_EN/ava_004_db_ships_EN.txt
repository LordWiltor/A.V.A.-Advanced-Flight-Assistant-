1. CLASS DICTIONARY, DOCKING RULES, AND TURRETS
================================================================================

### 1.1. Official Class Taxonomy, Evasion, and Generators
* `M1`: Heavy Carrier | `M2`: Heavy Destroyer | `M3 / M3+`: Heavy Fighter / Air Superiority | `M4 / M4+`: Medium / Heavy Interceptor | `M5 / M5+`: Light / Advanced Recon | `M6 / M6M`: Light / Heavy Corvette | `M7 / M7C / M7M`: Combat Frigate / Light Carrier / Missile & Boarding Frigate | `M8`: Tactical Bomber | `TL`: Heavy Station Transporter | `TS`: Commercial Freighter | `TP`: Personnel Transport | `TM`: Light Military Transport with Hangar | `GO`: Unique Goner Designs | `OWP`: Stationary Orbital Weapon Platform (Speed 0 m/s).
* **Lateral Strafe Physics (WASD Strafe in IS):** Small Ships (`M5` to `TM`): Factor 49,152 (~75 m/s) | Large Ships (`M6` to `M2`, `TL`): Factor 26,214 (~40 m/s).
* **Shield Recharge Rule (`Shield Gen in kW`):** If ship `Shield Gen` matches or exceeds the sum of `Gen Drain (kW)` of installed shields (`ava_003_db_weapons_EN.txt`), shields recharge at 100% of their `Max Recharge (kJ/s)`. If `Shield Gen` is lower, real recharge is `Shield_Gen * Shield_Efficiency` (`0.85` to `0.99`).
* **Marine Transport Capacity:** `M6`, `M6M`, and `TM`: **5 Marines** | `TP`: **10 Marines** | `M7`, `M7C`, `M7M`, `TL`, `M1`, `M2`: **20 Marines** | *Sirokos* (`Idx 262`, OTAS `M7M`): **30 Marines**.
* **ST Cargo and Factory-Fitted Equipment:** Class `ST` container on player ships is exclusive to `TL` transports and is the only class capable of carrying and deploying factories and Complex Construction Kits (`CCK`). The `Default Equip` column reflects built-in factory software and upgrades included free of charge when purchased at shipyards.

### 1.2. Station Docking Protocols and Hangar Exceptions
* **Docking at Conventional Factories:** Allowed only for light and medium ships (`M5`, `M4`, `M3`, `M8`, `TS`, `TP`, `TM`, and `M6`).
* **Capital Ship Docking Restrictions:** Ships of class `TL`, `M7`, `M7M`, `M1`, and `M2` CANNOT dock at standard factories or conventional Trading Stations; they operate exclusively at Equipment Docks, Shipyards, Military Outposts, Terran Military Bases, Corporate HQs, PHQ, Xenon Hub, and the Earth Torus.
* **Internal Hangar Compatibility:** Unless specifically noted, internal hangars accommodate exclusively `M5`, `M4`, `M3` fighters and light `GO` ships.
* **Unique Engine Exceptions:**
  * **Hyperion Vanguard (`SS_SH_P_M6_ADV`):** Features 2 internal hangar bays exclusively for `M5/M4/M3/GO` fighters (does not dock M6 or TS freighters).
  * **Goner Aran (`SS_SH_GO_MSY`):** Features 30 internal hangar bays for fighters (`M5/M4/M3/GO`) + **1 unique external docking clamp capable of docking 1 full capital ship** (`M1`, `M2`, `M7`, `TL`, `M6`, `TS`, or `TP`). *(Acquisition: Appears hidden off-radar exclusively in Unfocused Jumpdrive `UFJD` sectors; requires tagging with the spacesuit Repair Laser to register as hostile before boarding with Marines).*

### 1.3. Mathematical Interpretation of Turret Arcs and Conventions
* `Direction(Nx)`: Indicates 1 turret in that direction equipped with $N$ guns (e.g. `Front/Left/Right(8x)` = 1 Front turret of 8 + 1 Left turret of 8 + 1 Right turret of 8 = 24 total guns).
* `MxDirection(Nx)`: Indicates $M$ independent physical turrets covering that same arc (e.g. `2xFront(8x)` = 2 independent front turrets of 8 guns each = 16 total guns).
* **'Missile Launcher' Turrets and Critical M7M Frigate Exceptions:** On `M7M` frigates and `M8` bombers, indicates turret-controlled launch tubes for heavy torpedoes (`M_M8`, `M_M7M_TORP`, `M_M7M_AF`) or boarding pods (`M_POD`):
  * **No Boarding Pod Exception (`Aquilo`, `Skirnir`, `Senshi`):** The frigates `Aquilo` (`Idx 260`), `Skirnir` (`Idx 209`), and `Senshi` (`Idx 308`) **CANNOT fire Boarding Pods (`M_POD`)**; they are dedicated pure bombardment platforms.
  * **Pods-Only Exception (`Sirokos`):** The frigate `Sirokos` (`Idx 262`, purchasable at OTAS Rank +10) carries **30 Marines** (maximum in the game), but **ONLY fires Boarding Pods (`M_POD`)** and cannot load torpedoes or Flail missiles.
* **Turret Asymmetry and Container Class Restrictions in Aldrin and ATF Ships:**
  * *Rear turrets on Aldrin fighters:* The fighters *Spitfyre* (`Idx 224`), *#deca.cefa* (`Idx 227`), and *#deca.deaf* (`Idx 361`) mount experimental Aldrin weapons (`EEMPC/PMAML`) on main guns, but their **rear turrets require standard Solar System `EMPC`** (not sold in Aldrin).
  * *Container class incompatibility in Aldrin fighters:* Although the cockpit profile (`TCockpits`) of *Spitfyre* (`L`), *#deca.cefa* (`L`), *#deca.deaf* (`M`), and *#deca.fade* (`S`) lists the anti-fighter `PSSC`, this weapon requires **`XL` container class (`28/XL`)**, meaning **no Aldrin fighter can load it in its cargo bay** (only the *Springblossom* corvette with `XL` cargo can mount `PSSC`). Likewise, *#deca.fade* (`S` cargo) cannot load `PMAML` (`21/M`).
  * *Container class incompatibility in ATF corvettes (`Vidar` and `Vali`):* Their cockpits list the *Mobile Drilling System (`MDS`)*, but having **`L`** class cargo bays (`700/950/L` and `700/850/L`), they cannot load the `MDS` (`100/XL`), supporting only the *Tractor Beam (`TB`, `50/S`)*.
* **Directions:** `Front`, `Rear`, `Right`, `Left`, `Up`, `Down`.
