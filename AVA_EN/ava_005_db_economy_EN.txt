1. ECONOMIC RULES, COMPLEXES, AND INDUSTRIAL LOGISTICS
================================================================================

### 1.1. Production Scales and Capacity Factor
* `1x`: Standard base scale (Weapon Forges, Missile Factories, Shields, and Tech Fabs).
* `2x (M)`: Medium scale (Primary Bio, Refined Food, Crystal Fabs, M Mines, SPP M).
* `5x (L)`: Large scale (Primary Bio, Refined Food, Crystal Fabs, L Mines, SPP L).
* `10x (XL)`: Massive scale (Solar Power Plants XL).
* **Consumption Equivalence:** `1 Factory 5x (L)` exactly supplies `2.5 Factories 2x (M)` or `5 Equipment Forges/Factories 1x`.

### 1.2. Cost Rule and Complex Assembly (CCK Kits)
* **Complex Construction Kit Formula:** Joining $N$ physical stations into a unified complex strictly requires **$N - 1$ Complex Construction Kits (`CCK`)** (`SS_FAC_[A/B/P/S/T]_CCKIT`) at a fixed price of **`322,885 Cr`** each.
* **Hub Mechanics:** The first CCK joins the first 2 stations and deploys the docking node (*Complex Hub*). Each subsequent CCK connects 1 additional station to the existing complex.
* **Total Installation Budget:**
  $$\text{Total Cost} = \sum(\text{Price of the } N \text{ Stations}) + (N - 1) \times 322,885\text{ Cr}$$
* **Physical Pipe Limit and Chain Merging (>20 km):** Maximum distance of 20 km between any station and the Complex Hub (`SG_MAX_DISTANCE_BUILDCOMPLEX = 20,000 m`). To connect mines or factories located >20 km away without towing, a second intermediate Complex Hub can be deployed and both Hubs merged with 1 CCK (provided the two Hubs are <20 km apart; the first Hub selected takes precedence as the primary docking node).

### 1.3. Self-Sustaining Closed Loop
* **Universal Commonwealth Flow:** $\text{Silicon (Mine)} + \text{Bio} \longrightarrow \text{Food} \longrightarrow \text{Crystals} \longrightarrow \text{Energy Cells (SPP)} \longrightarrow \text{Powers entire complex}$.
* **Fixed Ratio (`1 Crystal = 138 Cells`) and Balance for SPP XL (`10x`):**
  * *At 100% Sun (`118 s` cycle):* Produces `+2,760` Cells and consumes `-20` Crystals every `118 s` (`84,203 Cells/h` and `610.2 Crystals/h`). With **5x Crystal Fabs M** (or `2x L`), **`600 Crystals/h` (`82,800 Cells/h` stable)** are produced, requiring **5x Food Fabs M** + **5x Bio Farms M** + **`375 Silicon Wafers/h`** (cumulative Silicon Yield sum **>= 50** in L Mines, e.g. 2x L Mines on yield 25 asteroids = `387 wafers/h`).
  * *At 150% Sun (`106 s` cycle, present in 177 sectors):* The SPP XL can produce up to `93,736 Cells/h` (`679.2 Crystals/h`). If maintained with **5x Crystal Fabs M**, the complex **does not collapse** and continues producing **`82,800 Cells/h`** (bottlenecked by the input of `600 Crystals/h`). To extract 100% output from the SPP XL at 150% Sun, install **6x Crystal Fabs M** (`720 Crystals/h`), **6x Food M**, **6x Bio M**, and **`450 Silicon Wafers/h`** (cumulative Silicon Yield **>= 58** in L Mines).
* **Terran Closed Loop Flow (`_PL` Factories in TUS 1):** $\text{Silicon (Mine)} + \text{Protein Paste} \longrightarrow \text{Terran MRE} \longrightarrow \text{Terran Crystals} \longrightarrow \text{Cells (Terran SPP)}$. Player-owned Terran factories **DO NOT consume Water or C-Rations** (they strictly require Energy Cells, Protein Paste, Terran MRE, and Ore/Silicon).
* **SPP Cycles by `Sun %` (`TSuns.txt`):** Sun 0% = `176 s` | Sun 100% = `118 s` | Sun 150% = `106 s` | Sun 300% = `90 s` (*Aldrin, Aldrin 2, TUS 1, A New Home*) | Sun 400% = `86 s` (*Akeela's Beacon*) | Sun 450% = `84 s` (*Harmony of Perpetuity*).
* **Player vs NPC Rule:** Player-owned factories **NEVER consume secondary resources**, nor do they consume Delexian Wheat in Cahoona Bakeries or Silicon in Missile/Torpedo Factories (those inputs are strictly demanded by NPC stations). On player-owned SPPs, Crystals are a **mandatory primary resource**.

### 1.4. Primary Racial Food Consumption (Per 1-minute base cycle at 1x scale)
* **Argon:** `-10 Meatsteak Cahoonas` (Base bio: `-3 Argnu Beef`).
* **Boron:** `-2.5 BoFu` (Base bio: `-0.83 BoGas`).
* **Paranid:** `-2 Soja Husk` (Base bio: `-12 Soya Beans`).
* **Split:** `-1.5 Rastar Oil` (Base bio: `-3 Chelts Meat`).
* **Teladi:** `-10 Nostrop Oil` (Base bio: `-15 Sunrise Flowers`).
* **Terran:** `-2.5 Terran MRE` (Base bio Player: `-10 Protein Paste`) or `-10 C-Rations` (NPC stations only).

### 1.5. TL Freight Logistics and Packaging Volumes (ST Cargo Bay)
Transporting and deploying stations strictly requires class **`ST`** cargo, exclusive to **`TL`** freighters. Approximate packaged volumes per station:
* **Complex Construction Kits (`CCK`):** `1,000 ST`.
* **Forges 1x (Lasers, Missiles, Shields, Tech):** `2,000` to `4,000 ST`.
* **Bio, Food, and Crystal Fabs M:** `2,000` to `3,500 ST`.
* **Bio, Food, and Crystal Fabs L:** `4,500` to `7,500 ST`.
* **Ore and Silicon Mines (M and L):** `10,000 ST`.
* **Solar Power Plants:** `SPP M` = `10,000 ST` | `SPP L` = `25,000 ST` | `SPP XL` = `40,000` to `50,000 ST`.

### 1.7. Player Docks and Trading Stations Logistics
Equipment Docks and Trading Stations constructed by the player DO NOT produce wares on their own; they operate as logistical redistribution storehouses regulated by the engine parameter `SG_DOCK_STORAGE_FACTOR` (`ava_007_db_mechanics_EN.txt`) and managed via Bonus Pack scripts (`CAG`, `CLS1/2`, `Dockware Manager`).
