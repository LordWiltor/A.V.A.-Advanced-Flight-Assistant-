1. GEOGRAPHIC RULES, LEGEND, AND TACTICAL DIRECTORY
================================================================================

### 1.1. Galactic Coordinates, Local Coordinates, and Security Levels
* **Galactic Grid `(X,Y)`:** Column `X` (West to East: `0` to `21`) | Row `Y` (North to South: `0` to `16`).
* **HUD Local Coordinates:** `X, Y, Z` expressed in real HUD kilometers (`km`). Engine conversion: `1 km = 500` internal units.
* **Sector Security:** `Core` (high military presence and strict notoriety requirement) vs `Border` (periphery, reduced patrolling).
* **Connection Link Format:** `Direction:DestinationSector(X,Y)[Coord_X,Y,Z_km]`.

### 1.2. Terran Transorbital Accelerator ([TOA]) Restrictions and Direct Jump Points
* **Difference between Jump Gates vs [TOA]:** Standard Jump Gates (`N:`, `S:`, `E:`, `W:`) accept direct jumps using a Jumpdrive. Terran Transorbital Accelerators (`[TOA]`) DO NOT allow direct jump targeting.
* **Terran/Aldrin Border Jump Gate Directory (Only direct access points for Jumpdrive):**
  1. *Heretic's End `(13,4)`* (Standard active gate from the start; Commonwealth link).
  2. *Asteroid Belt `(11,3)`* (Standard active gate from the start; only initial jump point inside the Solar System).
  3. *Neptune `(9,1)`* [Post-Aldrin Plot 2.0] (East gate constructed to TUS 2).
  4. *Aldrin 2 `(14,10)`* [Post-Aldrin Plot 2.0].
  5. *Terran Unknown Sector 1 / TUS 1 `(15,10)`* [Post-Aldrin Plot 2.0].
  6. *Terran Unknown Sector 2 / TUS 2 `(15,9)`* [Post-Aldrin Plot 2.0].
  7. *Terran Unknown Sector 3 / TUS 3 `(16,10)`* [Post-Aldrin Plot 2.0] (and its East connection to *Xenon Sector `(17,10)`*).
  *(Note: The Aldrin colony sector `(13,10)` lacks standard jump gates initially, but permits gateless point-to-point jumps to the sector center starting from Mission 5 of the Terran Plot. Access to the rest of the Solar System —Mars, Venus, The Moon, Earth— is achieved by jumping to Asteroid Belt or Neptune and cruising through the TOAs).*

### 1.3. Dynamic Plot Connection Rules and the (14,4) Anomaly
Connections marked in brackets with plot names (`[Aldrin Plot]`, `[HUB Plot]`, or `[ANH Plot]`) are **physically inactive or non-existent at game start**, spawning upon achieving the corresponding milestones in `ava_009_db_plots_EN.txt`. Sector **`(14,4) Harmony of Perpetuity`** (`maps/harmony_of_perpetuity.xml`, introduced in patch 3.1.1) lacks gates in the base map file and **remains inaccessible in standard installations**, as the mission script that spawned its North gate in *Elysium of Light `(14,5)`* was exclusive to the temporary *Steam Summer Camp 2011 (DLC A Sunny Place)* event.

### 1.5. Buildable Asteroids vs Debris Conventions
* `Mineral:Quantity(Tot:Yield = Breakdown)`: **Buildable Asteroids**. Support deployment of Mine M or L stations directly. Production rate scales strictly with `Yield / 25`.
* `[Debris: Mineral:Quantity(Tot:Yield)]`: **Mobile Mining Debris**. Cannot host stations; collected by busting them via *Mobile Drilling System (MDS)* or heavy ordnance and collecting with an *Ore Collector*.

### 1.6. Player Sector at (20,2) ('A New Home' Plot)
Upon completing the *A New Home* plot (`ava_009_db_plots_EN.txt`), sector `(20,2)` transfers ownership to the player. The player selects from 5 asteroid resource archetypes (Section 3) and 6 canonical designations: *Guiding Star*, *Twisted Skies*, *Cathedral of Xaar*, *CEO's Wellspring*, *Spires of Elusion*, or *Avarice*.
