|PHQ Operation|Time Required|Credit Cost|Resource Cost / Return|Engine Effect & Rules|

5. BOARDING MECHANICS (MARINES), BAILING, AND MOBILE MINING

### 5.1. Marine Boarding System
* **Boardable Ships:** `M6`, `M7`, `M7M`, `TL`, `M1`, `M2`, `TM`, and the special ship *Aran*. **Kha'ak ships CANNOT be boarded**. Fighters (`M5..M3`), bombers (`M8`), and freighters (`TS/TP`) cannot be boarded with Marines (their pilots surrender via *Bailing*).
* **Maximum Marine Capacity:** `M6` and `TM`: **5 Marines** | `TP`: **10 Marines** (spacewalk) | `M7`, `M7M`, `TL`, `M1`, `M2`: **20 Marines** | OTAS *Sirokos* (`M7M`): **30 Marines**. *(Note: The target ship accommodates up to its max capacity + 1 additional Marine during an assault, e.g. 6 in an M6 or 21 in capital ships).*
* **Boarding Pods (`M_POD`) and M7M Exceptions:** Exclusive to `M7M` frigates (5 marines per pod, speed `480 m/s`, hull `300 pts`). They double the hull-cutting penetration bonus compared to spacewalk boarding. **Critical Exception:** The `Aquilo` (OTAS), `Skirnir` (ATF), and `Senshi` (Yaki) frigates **CANNOT fire Boarding Pods**; the `Sirokos` (OTAS) **CAN ONLY fire Boarding Pods** and lacks missile ordnance to strip shields.
* **Recruitment (`Bioscanner`) and Training Costs:**
  * *Bioscanner (`SS_WARE_BIOSCANNER`):* Allows viewing skill stars prior to hiring Marines at Military Outposts or Pirate Bases (filtering out recruits with `< 2-3★` in Fighting).
  * *Training at Docks and Military Outposts:* Allows training **Mechanical**, **Engineering**, and **Hacking** via *Quick, Normal, and Advanced* courses. Training a Marine from `0★` to `5★` (`100 pts`) across all 3 trainable skills costs **`~1.2 to 1.5 million Cr`** in total. The **Fighting** skill CAN NEVER be trained at stations; it only increases by surviving real boarding operations.

#### The 4 Assault Phases and Race Difficulty Matrix
* **Phase 1 — Approach and Critical Shield Suppression:**
  * To launch Marines and throughout the entire hull-cutting duration (Phase 2), **target shields must remain below the critical threshold (`< 10%` on M6/TM, `< 8%` on M7, `< 5%` on M1/M2/TL; universal safe baseline: `1% to 4%`)**. If shields regenerate above this threshold before Marines breach the interior, the entire squad is electrocuted and killed on the hull.
* **Phase 2 — Hull Cutting (Mechanical):**
  * *Commonwealth and Pirates:* Requires **40 to 60 pts** (2-3★; easier with a Boarding Pod).
  * *Terran and ATF:* Heavy military plating; requires **>= 90 pts** (4-5★) and a Boarding Pod is mandatory.
  * *Xenon:* Extreme hull integrity; requires **100 pts** (5★) across all cutters and a Boarding Pod.
* **Phase 3 — Deck-by-Deck Combat (Fighting) and Damage Control (Engineering):**
  * *Fighting:* Governs survival floor by floor (from 2 decks on M6/TM up to 5 decks on M1/M2). On **Xenon** ships, internal automated resistance is lethal and demands reinforcing waves (21 to 30 Marines with 100 pts in Fighting).
  * *Engineering:* Governs how much damage friendly Marines inflict on the ship's hull and subsystems while clearing each deck. With **5★ (`100 pts`) Engineering on all Marines**, the ship is captured with **0% additional hull damage**.
* **Phase 4 — Core Hacking (Hacking):** Checks the average of the **2 best surviving hackers**:
  * *Commonwealth:* Requires **50 to 70 pts** (3★).
  * *Terran, ATF, and Xenon:* Requires **>= 90 to 100 pts** (5★).
* **Internal Defenses (Detectable via Freight Scanner + Bioscanner):**
  * *Hull Polarising Device:* Greatly increases the Mechanical requirement (makes spacewalk cutting impossible).
  * *Internal Sentry Lasers:* Drastically increase Marine casualties on each deck.
  * *Advanced Firewall Software:* Requires 100 pts in Hacking to prevent central mainframe failure. *(Tactical note: Sustained Ion Disruptor `ID` fire or continuous hull hits at 1% shield before launching can destroy these 3 internal defenses and onboard weaponry).*
* **Special Protocol to Locate and Board the *Goner Aran* (`SS_SH_GO_MSY`) and UFJD Sector Rules (`308_Sector_Management.xml`):**
  1. Jump to unknown sectors (`Internal coord 23,19`) using the **Unfocused Jumpdrive (`UFJD`)** in an `M7M` loaded with Boarding Pods and 20 Marines (**5★ in Engineering is mandatory**, as the *Aran* spawns with hull integrity between **`3% and 30%`**; Marines lacking max Engineering will destroy the ship from within). *(UFJD script note: on each unfocused jump, there is a ~10% probability that the drive surge destroys the player ship's **Transporter Device**, **Freight Scanner**, or **Triplex Scanner**; additionally, if the player has `<= 800` Energy Cells in the UFJD sector, the script automatically spawns 8 secret containers containing 100-500 Energy Cells).*
  2. Visual acquisition on the horizon (at 20-180 km), as the *Aran* is **invisible on radar and untargetable via the HUD**.
  3. Approach to `< 150 m`, exit ship in a **spacesuit**, and fire continuously with the **Repair Laser (`RL`)** for several seconds until the audio alert and combat music trigger (registering the ship internally as a hostile target).
  4. Return to the `M7M` and execute **Command Console -> Piracy -> Board Ship** (where the *Aran* will now be listed as a valid target) to launch Boarding Pods.

### 5.2. Pilot Bailing and Tactical Doctrine
* **Ships Subject to Bailing:** Fighters (`M5`, `M4`, `M3`), Bombers (`M8`), and Freighters (`TS`, `TP`).
* **Mathematical Bailing Condition:** Target shields must drop to **`< 1%`** and hull must be below **`87%`**.
* **Hidden Morale Variable (0 to 30):** Pilots with morale `>= 25` almost never eject. The engine runs a random check every time the ship takes hull damage under these conditions.
* **Tactical Capture Doctrine:** Maintain hull at ~80% and fire low-destructive, high-cadence burst weapons (`PBE`, `IRE`, `PAC`, or `Ion Disruptor`) to trigger dozens of morale checks without destroying the hull.
* **Claiming:** Approach to **`< 30 m`** in a spacesuit (or to **`< 50 m`** from the cockpit using illegal *System Override Software SOS*). The spacesuit mounts a free *Repair Laser* (`SS_LASER_REPAIR`) with an effective range of **200 m**.

### 5.3. Mobile Mining (Globals.txt)
* Fragmentation divisor: 30 (`SG_MM_YIELD_DIVISOR`). Empty debris percentage: 30%. Random minimum: 2 rocks per fragment. Projectile energy to break rock: 10,000. Debris collision destruction distance: 200 m.
