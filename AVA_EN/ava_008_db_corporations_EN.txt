1. CORPORATE SYSTEM MECHANICS (104 and 306_Corporation_Missions.xml)
================================================================================
* **Initial Contract Activation:** At `5 minutes` (`300s`) of game time, upon entering the sector where a Corporate Headquarters resides (`SS_DOCK_CORP_1..8`), if the station is non-hostile, its representative appears offering the Initial Offer.
* **Enrollment and Corporate Rank Scale:**
  * Completing the initial mission immediately awards **Rank +1 (1% progress)** with the corporation.
  * **Cooldown Between Missions (`L1M40 Wait`):** Between **`10 and 30 minutes`** of real game time after completing or failing a mission; the representative will transmit a message inviting the player back to the HQ.
  * **Rank Gain Per Mission (`L1M40 End`):** Each completed contract increases internal progress by **`20% to 33%`** (`min=rank/5`, `max=rank/3`), requiring **4 to 5 successful missions per rank level**.
  * **Penalties and Dismissal (`L1M40 Fired`):** Aborting or timing out deducts `-1 to -2` rank levels; the death of a VIP passenger deducts **`-5` rank levels**. If the internal score drops to **`<= -11`** (or if the HQ turns hostile), the player is fired, forfeits corporate rank, and the corporation takes **`1 to 2.5 hours`** (`3,600s` to `9,000s`) to reset its initial offer from scratch.
* **Reward Milestones (`L1M40 Reward`):**
  * Upon reaching **Ranks `+4`, `+7`, and `+10`**, the representative sends a special briefing message. Upon **docking personally at the HQ**, ownership of the exclusive ship docked at that station is transferred for free.
  * **OTAS Rank +10 Bonus (`L3M06 OTAS Reward Fix`):** In addition to receiving the *Skiron* corvette, the boarding frigate **`Sirokos` (`SS_SH_OTAS_HCF`)** (the only ship capable of carrying **30 Marines** and launching boarding pods) is permanently added to the purchase catalog of the **OTAS Shipyard in Legend's Home `(8,7)`**.

5. SPECIAL GAMESTART MISSIONS AND OVERTUNED SHIPS (305_Gamestart_Missions.xml)
================================================================================
The file `305_Gamestart_Missions.xml` executes unique mission scripts depending on gamestart ID:

### 5.1. Gamestart 106: Bankrupt Assassin
* **Script (`L3M05 106` -> `LUM10`):** Spawns Pirate bounty hunter wings (`Delay=600s`) jumping into border sectors to intercept the player. They cease permanently after surviving several consecutive waves.

### 5.2. Gamestart 114: Unholy Traitor
* **Script (`L3M05 114` -> `LUM10`):** The pilot begins in a stolen *Medusa Prototype*, hostile to both Paranid and Split. The script dispatches **15 Hard-difficulty Paranid bounty hunter waves** (`Amount=15`, `Delay=30s`) tracking the player across the galaxy.

### 5.3. Gamestart 110: Tormented Teladi — Overtuned Enhanced Kea
* **Objective and Time Limit (`L3M05 110`):** Amass **`2,250,000 Cr` in exactly 12 hours** (`43,200 s`) and deliver them to the marked Teladi Trading Station.
* **Rewards Upon On-Time Payment:**
  1. Unlocks Gamestart `111` (*Poisoned Paranid*).
  2. Awards the unique overtuned heavy fighter **`Enhanced Kea` (`SS_SH_T_M3P_ENH`)**: base speed `48 m/s` (normal max `15/15` tunings = `120 m/s`), but the script installs between **`18` and `33` Total Engine Tunings** (exceeding the standard limit of `15` by up to `+18`) and up to `+9` bonus Rudder Optimisations, reaching between **`134.4 m/s` and `206.4 m/s`**.
  3. Free cargo in hold: 32 Microchips, 18 Quantum Tubes, 250 Energy Cells, and 12 Fragmentation Bomb Launchers (FBL).

### 5.4. Gamestart 111: Poisoned Paranid — 250 m/s Hyperion Vanguard
* **Initial Restriction (`L3M05 111 Maintain Notoriety`):** Paranid reputation is locked at **`-10,000` (Rank -4)**, preventing docking or reputation gains while poisoned.
* **Objective and Time Limit (`L3M05 111`):** Pay **`25,000,000 Cr` within 75 hours** (`270,000 s`) to the Boron physician for the cure.
* **Rewards Upon Paying Within Deadline:**
  1. Restores Paranid reputation to `-10` (Rank -1), allowing normal reputation progression.
  2. **Overtuned `Hyperion Vanguard` (`SS_SH_P_M6_ADV`):** Base speed `67.7 m/s` (normal max `15/15` tunings = `169.1 m/s`), but the script installs between **`12` and `27` Total Engine Tunings** (exceeding the standard limit of `15` by up to **`+12`**) and up to **`+8`** bonus Rudder Optimisations, reaching between **`148.8 m/s` and `250.2 m/s`**. *(The exact number of tunings is determined by a random roll at the moment the 25,000,000 Cr are paid)*.
  3. **Overtuned `Advanced Perseus` (`SS_SH_P_M3_ADV`):** Awarded with up to `+8` Engine Tunings and `+5` Rudder Optimisations above factory max (reaching **~250-285 m/s**).

### 5.5. Critical Preservation Rules for Overtuned Ships
* **PHQ Reverse Engineering Prohibition:** **NEVER** submit a unique overtuned specimen (250 m/s *Hyperion Vanguard*, *Enhanced Kea*, or *Advanced Perseus*) for **Reverse Engineering** at the Player Headquarters. Reverse engineering destroys the overtuned ship, and the resulting blueprint only produces hulls with standard factory speed limits (`169.1 m/s` on the *Hyperion*). To obtain the *Hyperion* blueprint for serial production, board a standard *Hyperion* from Paranid patrol fleets with Marines and preserve your overtuned ship intact.
* **Equipment Dock / Trading Station Downgrade Trap:** When docked with an overtuned ship at any Equipment Dock or Trading Station, **NEVER press the left arrow key on the *Engine Tuning* or *Rudder Optimisation* upgrade bars**: the trade menu will sell any upgrades exceeding the factory cap and will never allow reinstalling them.
