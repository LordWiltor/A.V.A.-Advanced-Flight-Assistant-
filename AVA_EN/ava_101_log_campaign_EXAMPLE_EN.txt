🎯 1. CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES: THE ATLANTIS PROJECT (WSI)
================================================================================

### Ultimate Goal
* [ ] Establish the sovereign headquarters of *Wiltor Space Industries (WSI)* in **Unknown Sector `(8,8)`** (*Atlantis*), erecting it as a neutral sanctuary of advanced science, free trade, and military guardianship for the entire Commonwealth.

### Phase 1: Initial Liquidation, The "Iron Arrow", and the Satellite Stockpile
* [ ] Maximize engine (`346.2 m/s`) and rudder tunings on the *Iron Arrow* (Discoverer M5) after liquidating the 5 MJ shield at the equipment dock (`+20,775 Cr`, 100%) and the Mercury's hull at the Argon Prime shipyard (`~112,993 Cr`).
* [ ] Begin the journey northward (*Menelaus' Frontier `(3,0)`*) and claim the **Toucan Hauler** (`TP`, abandoned ship `#1`).
* [ ] **Satellite Stockpile Maneuver:** Send the *Toucan* on autopilot through the safe Boron/Argon corridor (*Queen's Space ➔ Kingdom End ➔ Three Worlds ➔ Argon Prime*) to dock at the **NPC Advanced Satellite Factory in Argon Prime `(1,3)`**.
* [ ] Remotely purchase each batch of advanced satellites produced by the factory, storing them in the Toucan's massive cargo bay (1,035 L) to prevent market saturation and accumulate the inventory for the future Network of Eyes.

### Phase 2: North-East Expedition, Layover at Omicron Lyrae, and the "Advanced Discoverer"
* [ ] Continue the expedition eastward along the northern route, claiming ships under the **Safe Docking and Local Stripping Protocol**:
  - *Weapon Stripping:* Sell weapons and shields for 100% average price at the nearest Equipment Dock.
  - *Common / Duplicate Ships:* Liquidate hulls at the nearest en-route shipyard (e.g., Mamba `#13` at Split or Teladi shipyards).
  - *Relic Ships (`🔒 RELIC`):* Disarm them at a local dock and leave them safely berthed in waystation hangars without risky ferry flights across the map (e.g., *Iguana Vanguard* in *Family Whi `(6,0)`*, *Harrier Sentinel* in *The Vault `(13,0)`*, and *Buzzard Hauler* in *Tkr's Deprivation `(15,3)`*).
* [ ] Upon reaching *Omicron Lyrae `(13,6)`*, temporarily pause the expedition to initiate the **Terran Plot** while keeping Combat Rank at 0 (*Harmless*).
* [ ] Complete the bridge mission to *Heretic's End*, Scene 1 (light patrols in Uranus/Saturn relying on Terran military escorts), and Scene 2 (tailing and base scanning in sector 12,5).
* [ ] Collect the 4 free *Rapier* fighters and dock in orbit of **Venus** to receive the **Advanced Discoverer (`SS_SH_A_M5_ADV`)**.
* [ ] Cruise back through the TOAs to the Commonwealth and transfer command to the Advanced Discoverer as WSI's new scout ship (`437.5 m/s`) to sweep the remaining southern relics at extreme speed.

### Phase 3: The "Network of Eyes" Tour and Enlisting "The Ten Custodians"
* [ ] With liquidity from the military salvage (`~2.5 to 3.5 million Cr`), acquire the **first 2 `Mistral` freighters (`SS_SH_OTAS_TS`)** in Legend's Home equipped with Trade Command Software MK3.
* [ ] **Trader Training Doctrine:**
  - *Levels 1 to 6:* **Sector Trader (ST)**.
  - *Level 6-7:* Restart as **Local Trader (LT)** with a 1 to 2 jump radius in safe core sectors (*Empire's Edge*, *Queen's Space*, or *Seizewell*).
  - *Level 12:* Activate **Universe Trader (UT)** only when the pilot autonomously equips their own Jumpdrive and Fighter Drones.
* [ ] Leverage passive trade to build notoriety to Rank +5 (1,000 pts) and purchase all **5 official Commonwealth Police Licenses**.
* [ ] Send the *Iguana Vanguard* (187 m/s) to dock at **TerraCorp HQ in Home of Light `(1,4)`**.
* [ ] **Crucial Telemetry Step (Bioscanner):** Purchase and install the **Bioscanner (`SS_WARE_BIOSCANNER`, 2,945 Cr)** at TerraCorp to inspect the innate Fighting stars of potential recruits.
* [ ] Transfer the entire stock of Advanced Satellites accumulated in the *Toucan's* cargo hold to the *Iguana Vanguard*.
* [ ] Embark on WSI's grand tour through the western galactic quadrant at the helm of the *Iguana Vanguard*:
  - **Deploying the Network of Eyes:** Drop 1 Advanced Satellite in every core and border sector of Argon, Boron, Teladi, Paranid, and Split for commercial coverage and early threat warning.
  - **Elite Recruitment:** Use the Bioscanner at en-route military outposts to hire 8 marines with high innate Fighting skill (2 Argon, 2 Split, 2 Paranid, and 2 Teladi).
  - **Boron Quantum Rescue:** Divert to a Pirate Rehabilitation Facility (*Danna's Chance `(4,6)`* or *Mi Ton's Refuge `(9,11)`*) to buy the freedom of **2 Boron quantum scientists**.
* [ ] Berth the Iguana Vanguard at the **Marine Training Barracks in Home of Light `(1,4)`** and remotely fund continuous training for The Ten Custodians until achieving **full 5 stars (100 pts) in Hacking, Mechanical, and Engineering**.

### Phase 4: Corporate Triad, Fleet of 10 Mistrals, and Opening the HUB
* [ ] Execute corporate contracts for the Argon Triad (**OTAS, Jonferco, and TerraCorp**), staggering their 10-30 minute mission cooldowns to unlock their exclusive ship prototypes.
* [ ] Reinvest trade and corporate dividends until establishing the **dedicated fleet of 10 Level 25 Mistral UTs**, reaching **45,000,000 Cr** in the WSI treasury.
* [ ] Outfit the expeditionary fleet: mothership **OTAS Zephyrus (TM)** and the **Golden Squadron** in a combined flight configuration:
  - Bay 1: *Advanced Discoverer* (Supersonic reconnaissance at 437 m/s).
  - Bay 2: *Advanced Eclipse M3+* (Personal squadron leader).
  - Bay 3: Standard *Argon Eclipse M3+* (Heavy escort and tanker carrying 250 Energy Cells in cargo hold).
  - Bay 4: *OTAS Solano M4+* (High-speed interceptor at 210 m/s mounting PRG cannons).
* [ ] Upon reaching **Rank +10 with OTAS**, unlock the **OTAS Sirokos (`SS_SH_OTAS_HCF`)** at the Legend's Home Shipyard: acquire it as WSI's definitive heavy assault flagship (30 marine berths and 480 m/s Boarding Pod tubes).
* [ ] As soon as Boron Rank +8 (*King's Knight*) is achieved, immediately initiate the **Xenon Hub (HUB) Plot** with Mahi Ma.
* [ ] **Rapid Recruitment of HUB Logisticians in Herron:** Purchase the freighters assigned to the HUB Plot equipped with CLS2 software and dock them at the **Free Argon Trading Station in Herron's Nebula `(1,2)`** before configuring their waypoint routes, taking advantage of the pilot union exploit to directly recruit veteran Rank 4 pilots (*Cargo Messenger*) capable of delivering Teladianium, Ore, and Silicon Wafers using jumpdrives immediately.

### Phase 5: Founding of Atlantis, Closed-Loop Megacomplexes, and PHQ Deployment
* [ ] Claim sovereignty over **Unknown Sector `(8,8)`** (*Atlantis*), deploy its perimeter grid of Advanced Satellites, and build the initial Equipment Docks and Trading Stations for logistical storage.
* [ ] Deploy the **Atlantis Defense Fleet**:
  - Perimeter ring of 10 to 15 **Lasertowers** guarding the East (*New Income*) and West (*Montalaar*) gate approaches.
  - **1x OTAS Skiron (M6M)** assigned to OOS sector patrol.
  - **1x Argon Cerberus (M7)** armed with Flak Artillery Arrays (FAA) for heavy deterrence.
  - In the late-game: **1x OTAS Boreas (M2)** anchored as the sector's permanent bastion.
* [ ] Construct self-sustaining closed-loop industrial megacomplexes on the **yield 55 Silicon asteroid**:
  - **Space Fuel Distillery** complex (exploiting the lawless jurisdiction of the unknown sector for massive sales to Argon clients without police interference).
  - **Space Weed Bliss Place** complex (supplying the massive illicit demand of the adjacent Teladi border in *New Income*).
* [ ] **Recruitment of Commercial Agents in Herron:** Route freighters assigned to the sale and distribution of Atlantis Space Fuel and Space Weed to the **Free Argon Trading Station in Herron's Nebula `(1,2)`** to hire them as veteran-grade **Commercial Agents (CAG)**, ensuring immediate Jumpdrive usage from their first trade run.
* [ ] Siphon surplus industrial output from Atlantis to complete the final phases of the HUB (specifically the 75,000 Microchips).
* [ ] After completing the mandatory 6-hour post-HUB wait, start the **Bala Gi's Legacy (HQ Plot)**: jump in with the **OTAS Sirokos** and launch **The Ten Custodians in 480 m/s Boarding Pods** to capture the pirate *Orca* TL with **0% engineering hull damage**.
* [ ] Deploy the **WSI Headquarters (`SS_DOCK_P_HQ`)** in Atlantis, initiating serial production of military fleets and reverse-engineering all harvested relics.
