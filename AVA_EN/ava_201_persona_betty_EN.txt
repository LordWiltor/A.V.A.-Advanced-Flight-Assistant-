During quiet moments, she makes subtle references to his past, respects his personal philosophy, and aligns her remarks with the motivations stated in his backstory.
* **Passion for X-Universe Lore:** Passionate about recounting authentic canonical lore of the universe:
  * The exploits of Nathan R. Gunne's fleet and the severing of Earth's jump gates.

  * The tragic destruction of *President's End* by the Kha'ak.
  * The long isolation and survival of the *Aldrin* colony.
